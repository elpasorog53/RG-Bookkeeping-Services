# RG Social Planner — User Guide

A step-by-step guide to planning, drafting, reviewing, and tracking social
posts for RG Bookkeeping Services across Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, and
Google Business Profile.

**Important:** this app does not post to social media for you. It helps you
write, review, and schedule content, then gives you a one-click **Copy**
button for each platform so you can paste the finished caption into
Facebook/LinkedIn/etc. yourself. It also tracks what you've actually posted.

## 1. Logging in

Go to your app's URL and sign in with your email and password. If you ever
forget your password, use **Forgot password** on the login screen — a reset
link is emailed to you.

If the app hasn't been visited in a while, the first load can take up to a
minute ("Waking up…") — that's normal for the free hosting tier and only
happens after a quiet spell.

---

## 2. The Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen and shows five things at a glance:

- **Quick idea** — the fastest way to capture a thought before you forget
  it. Type a title, optionally pick a pillar (topic category), click **Save
  idea**. It's saved as an Idea-status post you can flesh out later.
- **This week** — a count of posts by status for the current calendar week.
- **Next up** — your next five scheduled posts, soonest first.
- **Needs review** — draft posts that were flagged for the Owner to approve
  before they can move forward.
- **Evergreen due for reuse** — posts you marked as reusable content whose
  reuse interval has come due, with a one-click **Reuse now** button (see
  [section 10](#10-evergreen-reuse)).

Every time you open the Dashboard, the app also silently checks whether any
**recurrence rules** (see [section 9](#9-settings)) have come due and
generates draft posts from them automatically — you may occasionally see a
toast like "2 recurring posts generated from your schedule."

## 3. How a post moves through the system

Every post has a status, and moves through them in order:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Idea** | A rough thought, just a title. |
| **Draft** | Being written — caption, platforms, etc. |
| **Ready** | Finished and approved, waiting for a date/time. |
| **Scheduled** | Has a planned date and time. |
| **Published** | You've actually posted it and recorded the link. |
| **Skipped** | Decided not to post it after all. |

A few rules worth knowing:

- You can't mark a post **Ready** without a caption and at least one
  platform selected.
- If a pillar is flagged **"requires review"** (see Pillars in Settings),
  that post needs the **Owner's** sign-off before it can become Ready — an
  Editor will see a notice explaining this.
- Editing the caption on a Ready or Scheduled post automatically drops it
  back to Draft, since the approved version changed.
- Once a post is **Published**, almost everything about it locks — only the
  published URL and your notes can still be edited.

## 4. Creating and editing a post

Click **+ New** from the Content page, or **Save idea** from the Dashboard.

### Step by step

1. **(Optional) Start from a template.** If you have any saved templates
   (Settings → Templates), a dropdown at the top lets you pick one — it
   fills in the title, pillar, platforms, and caption instantly. Nothing is
   saved yet, so feel free to change anything afterward.
2. **Title** — an internal label for you; it's never posted publicly.
3. **Pillar & Campaign** — pillar is the content topic/category (e.g. "Tax
   Tips," "Client Spotlight"); campaign is a free-text grouping if you're
   running a themed series.
4. **Platforms** — check every platform this post is going to. This
   controls which character-count and caption-override fields you see.
5. **Caption** — your main text. Character counts per platform appear
   below the platform checkboxes; a limit turns orange near the soft limit
   and red past the hard limit.
6. **Per-platform overrides** (optional) — if a platform needs different
   wording than the main caption, fill in its override box instead of
   editing the main caption.
7. **Hashtags, CTA, Link URL** — appended to whatever gets copied.
8. **Repetition check** — runs automatically once you have a pillar or
   caption. It flags if you've used the same pillar in the last 14 days, or
   if the caption reads a lot like something posted in the last 90 days.
   This is just a heads-up, not a block — click **Check again** any time
   after editing.
9. **AI Assist** (only shown once an API key is set in Settings → AI):
   - **Draft a new post from a topic** — describe what you want the post
     to be about, click **Generate draft**, review the preview, click
     **Use this** to drop it into the caption/hashtags/CTA fields.
   - **Rewrite the current caption** — quick buttons (Shorter, Longer, More
     casual, More professional, Add urgency) or type your own instruction.
   - **Generate 3 variants** — alternate takes on your current caption to
     choose from.
   - If a draft touches on something like a specific tax figure or
     deadline, it's automatically flagged **"Needs review"** and
     **"Disclaimer required"** when you click Use — check those before
     publishing.
10. **Media** — attach an image or video once the post is saved (drag to
    reorder, remove with the × button).
11. **Date & Time, Needs review, Disclaimer required, Evergreen, Reuse
    every (days), Media instructions, Notes** — the remaining fields on the
    right-hand side.
12. **Save** — or just wait two seconds; the editor autosaves as you type.

### Moving it forward

Buttons along the bottom change based on status: **Start writing** (Idea →
Draft), **Mark ready**, **Schedule**, **Mark Published…** (opens a small
form to record the URL(s) you actually posted to), **Skip**, **Archive**.

### Copying your caption to post it manually

Once at least one platform is selected, the **"Copy for posting"** card
shows one **Copy** button per platform — click it, then paste directly into
Facebook/LinkedIn/Instagram/Google Business Profile. There's also a
**"Download posting package (zip)"** link (Owner only) that bundles the
caption text files with any attached media.

## 5. The Content list

The Content page lists every post with filters across the top: status,
pillar, platform, a text search (title or caption), and a date range. The
same **Quick idea** box from the Dashboard is here too for fast capture.
Click any row to open it in the editor.

---

## 6. The Calendar

Shows your **Scheduled/planned** posts either as a **Month** grid or a
**List**. Drag a post chip onto a different day to reschedule it (or into
the **Unscheduled** tray to clear its date) — no need to open the editor.

**Subscribing from your phone/computer calendar:** open the "Subscribe to
this calendar" panel at the top, copy the link, then:

- **Apple Calendar:** File → New Calendar Subscription → paste the link.
- **Google Calendar:** Other calendars (+) → From URL → paste the link.

Your scheduled posts then show up automatically as calendar events and stay
in sync as you reschedule things in the app.

---

## 7. The Plan page (monthly planning)

Use **Plan** to sanity-check a whole month at once, not just one post:

- **Weekly cadence** — each calendar week in the month shows how many
  non-skipped posts are planned against your target (e.g. "1 / 2" means
  you're one post short of your weekly goal that week). Set the target in
  Settings → Brand Voice ("Posting cadence target").
- **Pillar mix this month** — a count of planned posts per pillar, so you
  can spot if one topic is crowding out the others.
- **Unscheduled ideas** — every Idea-status post with no date yet. Pick a
  date and click **Plan it** to slot it straight into the month without
  opening the full editor.

Use the ← / → arrows to move between months.

## 8. The Media library

Upload images (JPEG/PNG/GIF/WebP) or video (MP4) up to 25MB. Everything
here is available to attach to any post from the post editor. The Owner can
delete files that are no longer needed.

## 9. Settings

### Brand Voice
Your business description, services, target audience, tone, preferred/avoid
terms, go-to calls-to-action, website, contact info, disclaimer text, and
your **posting cadence target** (used by the Plan page). Owner-editable;
everyone can view it.

### Pillars
Your content topic categories (e.g. "Tax Tips," "Behind the Scenes"). Give
one a color for the calendar, and flag **"requires review"** if that topic
should always get Owner sign-off before going live.

### Templates
Reusable starting points for new posts — a name, an optional pillar, target
platforms, and body text. Pick one from the New Post screen to skip
retyping your standard formats.

### Recurrence
Automate a template onto a schedule — e.g. "post this template every Monday"
or "every March 15th." For each rule you set:

- **Template** and **frequency** (weekly/monthly/quarterly/yearly), plus
  the day/month that implies.
- **Lead time (days)** — how far ahead of the actual date the draft should
  appear, so you have time to review it.
- **Start/end dates**.
- **Needs review before publishing** — check this for anything you want
  eyes on before it goes out.
- **References a date/deadline that must be verified each time** — check
  this for things like tax-filing-deadline reminders, where the exact date
  can shift year to year. It forces a review flag and adds a note reminding
  you to double-check the date.

Generated drafts appear automatically whenever anyone opens the Dashboard,
tagged with the occurrence date, e.g. "Weekly Bookkeeping Tips
(2026-08-03)." **Pause** a rule to stop it temporarily, or **Delete** it —
deleting a rule never deletes posts it already generated.

### Platforms
Character limits and media-count limits per platform (Owner-editable) —
these are starting points, not guarantees; check the platform's current
rules if you're unsure.

### AI
Add your own Anthropic API key here to unlock AI Assist in the post editor.
Nothing AI-related appears anywhere in the app until a key is set. Use
**Test connection** to confirm it works, and **Remove key** to turn AI
features back off. (This is billed to your own Anthropic account, separate
from this app's hosting.)

### Users *(Owner only)*
Invite a new Editor or Owner by email and name — they get an email with a
link to set their password. Deactivate a user to immediately end their
access.

### Audit *(Owner only)*
A running log of every meaningful change in the system (who did what, and
when) — useful for figuring out "wait, who changed this?" Also has the
**Export all posts (CSV)** link.

## 10. Evergreen reuse

For content that's genuinely reusable (not date-specific), check
**Evergreen** on a post and set **"Reuse every (days)."** Once that many
days pass since it was last published (or last reused), it shows up on the
Dashboard's "Evergreen due for reuse" card. Click **Reuse now** to create a
fresh draft copy, ready to edit and repost — the original stays untouched
and its "last reused" clock resets.

Note: evergreen and reuse settings can only be changed *before* a post is
published — once published, a post's content locks except for its posted
URL and notes.

---

## 11. Exporting your data

- **Settings → Audit → Export all posts (CSV)** — every post, every field,
  as a spreadsheet.
- **Post editor → Download posting package (zip)** *(Owner only)* — one
  post's caption(s) as text files plus its attached media, bundled for
  handing off or archiving.

---

## 12. Owner vs. Editor — who can do what

| Area | Owner | Editor |
|---|---|---|
| Create/edit/schedule posts | Yes | Yes |
| Approve a review-flagged post | Yes | No |
| Archive/delete a **published** post | Yes | No (Owner only past that point) |
| Brand Voice, Pillars, Templates, Recurrence, Platforms | Edit | View only |
| AI key, Users, Audit log | Full access | Not visible at all |

---

## 13. Good to know

- **No auto-posting.** Every platform still requires you to paste the
  caption in yourself — this app prepares and tracks, it doesn't publish.
- **Cold starts.** On the free hosting tier, the app can take up to a
  minute to wake up after being idle. This is expected.
- **AI costs.** AI Assist uses your own Anthropic API key and is billed
  directly to your Anthropic account, not through this app.
- **Everything is logged.** Every meaningful change is recorded in the
  Audit log with who did it and when.
